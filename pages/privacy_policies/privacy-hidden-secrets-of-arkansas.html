Hidden Secrets of Arkansas Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Hidden Secrets of Arkansas

Effective October 21, 2026

Hidden Secrets of Arkansas ("the app") is made by Irie Development ("we," "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses, who receives it, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we don't run servers that store your personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device identifiers, if you allow tracking
  • your approximate location, estimated from your IP address
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them and with the app
  • crash reports, performance data and other diagnostic data

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and improve its services. If you allow it, Google also uses it to personalize ads. Google's handling of this data is described in its Privacy Policy and on its page about how Google uses data from apps that use its services. We do not sell your data, and we do not receive your advertising identifier or ad data in a form that identifies you.

Your ad choices

  • Tracking permission: The app asks for permission before it uses your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where it's required): The app shows a consent screen from Google before showing ads. You can change your choice anytime from the Privacy Choices option in the app's Settings.
  • Remove Ads: A one-time in-app purchase removes ads, and with them the ad-related data collection.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location while you're using it. It uses it to show the places nearest you, how far away they are, directions, and the weather where you are. To get weather, the app sends your location to Apple's WeatherKit service. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy and doesn't use it to identify you. We don't store your location or send it to anyone else. The app's widget and Apple Watch app use the same location on your devices. You can turn off location access anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. The app will still work, but it won't show you what's nearby.

Microphone and recordings

The Spirit Box and EVP tools use the microphone, with your permission, to measure sound levels and record audio clips. Recordings are saved only on your device. We never upload or receive them. You can rename or delete them in the app, and they're removed if you delete the app. If you choose to share a recording, it goes only where you send it. You can turn off microphone access in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells us only that the purchase is active.

Data retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the app. Data that Google collects for ads is kept under Google's retention policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the use of your personal data. Because we don't hold personal data on our servers, the fastest way to control it is with the choices above. For data Google holds, you can also use Google's own tools. You're welcome to contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
